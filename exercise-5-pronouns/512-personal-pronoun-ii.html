1. When you have finished your work, please pass _____ (it, them) up to _____ (I, me).

2. Jane has a cat; _____ (it, she) likes to play with _____ (she, her, it).

2. Jane has a cat; she likes to play with it .

3. I met Andy yesterday. _____ (He, I) invited _____ (him, I, me) to his house.

4. James dreamed he saw a dinosaur, so _____ (he, it) ran as fast as _____ (he, it) could.

4. James dreamed he saw a dinosaur, so he ran as fast as he could.

5. I met Sally with her pet parrot yesterday. _____ (She, I) took a walk with _____ (she, her, I, me).

5. I met Sally with her pet parrot yesterday. I took a walk with her .

6. I heard _____ (her, she) talking to _____ (they, them) about _____ (I, me).

6. I heard _____ (her, she) talking to _____ (they, them) about _____ (I, me).

6. I heard her talking to them about me .

7. John was the only boy _____ (they, them) chose to play in the team with _____ (they, them).

7. John was the only boy _____ (they, them) chose to play in the team with _____ (they, them).

7. John was the only boy they chose to play in the team with them .

8. The dark clouds warned _____ (we, us) that the rain would soon catch _____ (we, us) up.

8. The dark clouds warned _____ (we, us) that the rain would soon catch _____ (we, us) up.

8. The dark clouds warned us that the rain would soon catch us up.

9. Janet was sorry that _____ (she, her) was not at home when _____ (they, them) called to see _____ (she, her).

9. Janet was sorry that _____ (she, her) was not at home when _____ (they, them) called to see _____ (she, her).

9. Janet was sorry that she was not at home when they called to see her

10. Does _____ (she, her) know that _____ (I, me) was absent?

10. Does _____ (she, her) know that _____ (I, me) was absent?

10. Does she know that I was absent?

11. Why didn't _____ (he, him) tell _____ (we, us) about _____ (they, them) when _____ (he, him) came yesterday?

11. Why didn't _____ (he, him) tell _____ (we, us) about _____ (they, them) when _____ (he, him) came yesterday?

11. Why didn't he tell us about them when he came yesterday?

12. It is likely that _____ (we, us) shall be in time to see _____ (she, her) when _____ (she, her) arrives.

12. It is likely that _____ (we, us) shall be in time to see _____ (she, her) when _____ (she, her) arrives.

12. It is likely that we shall be in time to see her when she arrives.

13. Please give _____ (I, me) the message for _____ (he, him)

13. Please give _____ (I, me) the message for _____ (he, him).

13. Please give me the message for him .

14. My friend and _____ (I, me) went to see _____ (she, her) to ask _____ (she, her) about her brother.

14. My friend and _____ (I, me) went to see _____ (she, her) to ask _____ (she, her) about her brother.

14. My friend and I went to see her to ask her about her brother.

15. The man gave Dan and _____ (I, me) some free samples which _____ (he, him) had with _____ (he, him).

15. The man gave Dan and _____ (I, me) some free samples which _____ (he, him) had with _____ (he, him).

15. The man gave Dan and me some free samples which he had with him .

16. I remember that _____ (they, them) brought _____ (we, us) some pineapples from their home.

16. I remember that _____ (they, them) brought _____ (we, us) some pineapples from their home.

16. I remember that they brought us some pineapples from their home.

17. _____ (He, Him) could not sleep because _____ (they, them) made so much noise. _____ (It, They) kept _____ (he, him) awake.

17. _____ (He, Him) could not sleep because _____ (they, them) made so much noise. _____ (It, They) kept _____ (he, him) awake.

17. He could not sleep because they made so much noise. It kept him awake.

18. Do you think that Michael and _____ (I, me) can catch _____ (they, them) if _____ (they, them) come this way?

18. Do you think that Michael and _____ (I, me) can catch _____ (they, them) if _____ (they, them) come this way?

18. Do you think that Michael and I can catch them if they come this way?

19. Don't tell _____ (she, her) about _____ (I, me)

19. Don't tell _____ (she, her) about _____ (I, me).

19. Don't tell her about me .

20. My brother told John and _____ (I, me) that _____ (we, us) must help _____ (he, him) wash the car.

20. My brother told John and _____ (I, me) that _____ (we, us) must help _____ (he, him) wash the car.

20. My brother told John and me that we must help him wash the car.
